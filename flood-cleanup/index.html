Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, Salinas Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Salinas to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Salinas

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.
